The plugin resolves conflicts for real: keep mine, keep incoming, keep both as a side-by-side copy, manual merge and resolve all, without ever losing the local copy. Conflicts resolved from the web app or another device are followed, and conflicts this device can't resolve are shown with a hint to resolve them elsewhere. The snapshot's open conflicts carry the file's current version.
